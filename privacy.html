2. Data we may process

At the draft date, the website has no account, contact form, booking, checkout or payment feature. We may still process:

  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, date and time, referring page and security logs generated by the hosting or content-delivery services.
  • Communications: your name, contact details and message if you contact us by email, phone, social media or another channel.
  • Event media: photographs or recordings that may include attendees if event photography is conducted.

Data comes from you, your device, our service providers or authorised event photographers. Providing data directly is generally voluntary. If you do not provide information needed to answer a request, we may be unable to respond.

3. Cookies, analytics and external services

The current website code does not set cookies, use browser storage, run analytics or use advertising trackers. The site loads software from jsDelivr, which receives technical connection data needed to deliver that software. Google Maps receives data only if you choose to open the directions link.

The production host or content-delivery provider may keep essential security and request logs under its own privacy terms.

We may use personal data to:

We will seek consent where required and otherwise process data only as permitted by applicable law. We do not use website visitors' data for direct marketing at launch. If that changes, marketing messages will include a way to opt out.
