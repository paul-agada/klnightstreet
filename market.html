The Street

A culinary haven that comes alive after dark.

Nestled in Kuchai Lama, KL Night Street is where the city exhales after sunset — tents go up, woks fire up, and buskers set up their mics as the smell of charcoal and spice fills the row.

It's not one cuisine, it's a whole street of them: Palestinian, Malay, and every fried snack in between, all shoulder to shoulder. Open until 4AM, every single night.
