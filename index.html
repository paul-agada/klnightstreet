The Street

A culinary haven that comes alive after dark.

Nestled in Rampai Business Park, KL Night Street is where the city exhales after sunset — tents go up, woks fire up, and buskers set up their mics as the smell of charcoal and spice fills the row. That photo you just walked past? Still hanging up ahead, over the street.

It's not one cuisine, it's a whole street of them: Palestinian, Malay, and every fried snack in between, all shoulder to shoulder. Open until 12AM, every single night.
