The Street

A culinary haven that comes alive after dark.

Nestled in Kuchai Lama, KL Night Street is where the city exhales after sunset — stalls light up, woks fire up, and the street fills with the smell of Tom Yum and charcoal smoke.

It's the kind of place you come for a proper late-night dinner, or just to lepak and chill with friends until the early hours. Open until 4AM, every single night.
